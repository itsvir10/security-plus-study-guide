# Resources

Study resources used while preparing for the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) exam.

## Video Course

- **[CyberCraft Training - Security+ Playlist (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zfwxSmL4n6w&list=PLUkY1OVVHzVljGOe8WAkKGc4GT8ZAKaav)** - a full free video course covering the SY0-701 objectives, including performance-based question (PBQ) walkthroughs.

## Practice Tests

- **[ExamCompass - Free Security+ Practice Tests](https://www.examcompass.com/comptia/security-plus-certification/free-security-plus-practice-tests)** - free, topic-by-topic quizzes covering every domain, useful for targeted review of a specific area.
- **[Dion Training - Security+ Unlimited Practice Exam Pack](https://www.diontraining.com/products/comptia-security-sy0-007-unlimited-practice-exam)** - scenario-based full-length practice exams, widely regarded as one of the closer simulations of the real exam's style and difficulty.
